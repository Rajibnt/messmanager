System Audit Statement

  • **Total Bazaar Cost** is the aggregate of all purchases directly recorded by mess members, serving as a credit (credit equals bazaar done).
  • **Total Fixed/Shared Costs** are equally split among all active registered members (`Shared Cost = Total Shared / Total Members`).
  • **Meal Cost** is computed by multiplying a member's individual monthly meal consumption by the dynamic meal rate.
  • **Positive net balances** indicate the member is in credit and should receive a refund from the cash box.
  • **Negative net balances** indicate the member is in debit and must immediately clear their outstanding balance by paying the manager.
